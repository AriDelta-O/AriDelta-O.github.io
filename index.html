About Me

I'm AriDeltaO. I do 3D art, mostly hard-surface stuff like mechs and mechanical design, plus environments. I work entirely in Blender.

I care a lot about clean topology and making things that look like they could actually work. I've done everything from mech lineups where every joint has a reason, to full-scale architectural scenes. The technical side matters just as much to me as making it look good.

I also worked on this portfolio site, customising the layout, structure, and how everything fits together. When I'm not modelling I'm usually messing with Arch Linux or trying to push Blender's node system in ways it probably wasn't designed for.
